Field notes

Pack & provisions

Brackenford

Trees, stone, berries and ore are renewable: each patch yields less as you pick it, goes dim when spent, and recovers over time. Woods and rock can be walked through.
Each town has plentiful goods (cheap to buy) and wanted goods (they pay a premium), and you must walk there to trade. If the pantry runs short, production drops by 75%.
Enemies slowly return to the wilds and to mines you have cleared; a dungeon refills only after every foe on its level is defeated.
The world runs in real time: enemies roam, workers and soldiers go about their day, and a new day dawns every 90 seconds (it pauses during dialogs and fights).
Move: WASD or arrow keys   Interact: E
Build a fishing hut or hunting camp for more pantry meals. A barracks sends soldiers on patrol to explore nearby land and drive off close enemies. They automatically use your best equipped blacksmith gear. Explore caves for metals and gems, delve into dungeons for foes and loot, and approach foxes to gather fur. Mine shafts lead down to deeper levels with richer seams and tougher creatures; each mine has its own depth limit. Build a lumber mill, mine, and tannery to hire workers. Their goods arrive each day; craft gear at the blacksmith with materials you own. Enemies share the same map marker, but each region has different foes; the stronger ones are rare and revealed only when combat begins.
